    How to Play the Game:
    1. Click the "Start" button and observe which button blinks.
    2. Click the blinking button, then watch for the next button to blink.
    3. Repeat the sequence by clicking the buttons in the correct order as they blink.
    4. If a button is not blinks again, it means the same button is part of the sequence.
    5. If you click the wrong button, the game will restart. Click "Restart" to try again.

    Scoring:
    - Max Score = 10: Normal Brain
    - Max Score = 15: Good Brain
    - Max Score = 20: Nice Brain
    - Max Score = 25: Excellent Brain
    - Max Score > 30: Winner of the Game!
